Phase 3 Frontend Design Doc: Clinical Workflow1. Visual Strategy: "The Clean Clinic"Grid System: Use a strict 12-column grid with generous "Gutter" space (padding) to prevent the medical data from feeling cramped.Primary Accent: Navy Blue (#1E3A8A) for "Actionable" items (buttons, links).Secondary Accent: Slate Gray (#64748B) for "Secondary" info (metadata, timestamps).Data Visualization: Use simple Blue line charts on a White background for health trends.2. Core Modules & ComponentsA. Appointment Manager (Patient & Provider)The Calendar View: A minimalist, white-on-white calendar grid with 1px gray borders.Upcoming Slot: A simple blue dot or a thin blue border around the date.Selected State: Solid Navy Blue circle with white text.Booking Modal: A clean vertical form.Input: Date Picker, Time Dropdown (Blue text), and a "Reason for Visit" text area (Slate Black border).Status Badges: * Confirmed: Navy Blue text on light blue tint.Pending: Dark gray text on light gray tint.B. Record Previewer (The "Lightbox")Instead of downloading files, providers need to see them instantly.The Viewer: A full-screen "Modal" with a semi-transparent black background.The Document: Centered white container displaying the PDF or Image.Toolbar: Fixed at the top; contains a "Close" (X) icon and a "Download" icon in minimalist black/white.C. Health Timeline VisualizationThis is the visual "Star" of the dashboard.The Vertical Track: A thin, 2px gray vertical line running down the center or left of the screen.Timeline Nodes: Small blue circles representing events.Node Content: Date (Bold Black), Event Title (Blue), and a small "View" link.Icons: Use simple monochrome icons (e.g., a tiny "Plus" for records, a "Clock" for appointments).3. Navigation & FlowSidebar Addition: Add "Appointments" and "Timeline" to the navigation.Breadcrumbs: Implement a simple breadcrumb system at the top (e.g., Patients > John Doe > Timeline) in Slate Gray to help providers navigate deep clinical paths.4. Logic & Data HandlingTimeline Fetcher: The frontend should call the GET /api/patients/:id/timeline endpoint you designed in the backend Phase 3.Date Formatting: Use a library like date-fns or dayjs to ensure dates are consistent (e.g., "Oct 12, 2026") across all components.Loading States: Use "Skeleton Screens" (ghostly gray shapes) instead of spinning wheels to keep the minimalist feel while data loads.📊 Phase 3 Component InventoryComponentUI ElementsVisual PriorityCalendar GridWhite background, 1px Gray border, Blue accents.HighTimeline NodeSmall Blue circle, Bold Black header, Gray subtext.HighPreview ModalDark overlay, White center-frame, Black icons.MediumAppointment CardWhite card, Blue "Join" button for Telehealth.Medium
