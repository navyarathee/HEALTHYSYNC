 Phase 2 Frontend Design Doc: Privacy & Consent UI1. Aesthetic Guidelines (Minimalist Healthcare)Backgrounds: Pure White (#FFFFFF) or subtle Off-White (#F9FAFB).Primary Action: Deep Navy Blue (#1E3A8A) for buttons and active states.Typography: Slate Black (#111827) for high-readability headers.Borders: Thin, light-gray hairpins (1px solid #E5E7EB) to separate sections without adding clutter.Shadows: Very soft or non-existent (rely on borders and spacing for depth).2. Core Views & ComponentsA. Consent Management (Patient View)This screen should feel like a "Security Dashboard" where the user has total control.The Access Table: Use a clean, borderless table on a white background.Row 1: Dr. Sarah Jenkins — Cardiology — Active until Oct 12 — [Revoke Access (Ghost Button)].Empty State: If no providers have access, show a simple illustration or text: "Your data is currently private. No providers have active access."Grant Access Button: A floating action button (FAB) or a prominent navy blue button: + Authorize Provider.B. Access Reason Modal (Provider View)This is the "Gatekeeper" component you’ll trigger before a provider can see sensitive data.Design: A centered white modal with a slate black header: "Access Verification Required".The "Why" Dropdown: A simple, minimalist select menu.Emergency Toggle: A subtle toggle at the bottom: "This is a medical emergency" (which changes the button color to a warning red or high-contrast black).C. Transparency Feed (Audit Logs)A vertical timeline using simple dots and lines.Entry: [Icon] Viewed Blood Report — Dr. Aris — 2 mins ago.Color Coding: Use blue for standard access and a subtle gray for logins. Avoid "loud" colors unless it’s an unauthorized access alert.3. Revised Navigation (The "Shell" Update)Since you finished Phase 1, ensure your Sidebar matches this new palette:Inactive Links: Slate Gray.Active Link: Deep Blue text with a small blue vertical bar on the left (no background highlight, to keep it minimalist).Profile Section: A simple, round avatar in the bottom-left with the user's name in Slate Black.4.
