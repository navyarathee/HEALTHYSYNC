 Phase 5 Frontend Design Doc: Analytics & Hardening1. Visual Strategy: "The Command Center"Background: White or very light gray (#F9FAFB) to separate the "Admin" area from the "Clinical" area.Data Visualization: Use Navy Blue for primary data trends and Slate Black for axes and labels. Avoid multi-colored pie charts; use varying shades of blue or simple bar graphs to maintain the minimalist aesthetic.Security Alerts: Use Bold Black backgrounds with White text for critical security notifications to create a high-contrast "Warning" state without breaking your color palette.2. Core Modules & ComponentsA. Governance & Analytics Dashboard (Admin View)This is a high-level overview of the entire HealthSync ecosystem.Metric Tiles: Simple white boxes with a 1px gray border.Top: Large Black Number (e.g., 1,240).Bottom: Small Blue Label (e.g., Active Patients).System Usage Graph: A minimalist blue area chart showing "Data Requests over 24 Hours."Emergency Access Table: A list of every time a provider used the "Emergency Bypass."Style: Bold the "Reason" in Black to make it stand out for auditors.B. Advanced Identity: 2FA & Session ManagementOTP Entry Screen: A stark white screen with six empty black-bordered boxes. When a number is entered, the box border turns Navy Blue.Session List: In the user's settings, show a list of "Active Devices."Action: A simple "Log Out" link in Blue next to each device.Security Banner: A thin black bar at the top of the screen if 2FA is not yet enabled: "Secure your records. Enable Two-Factor Authentication." (White text, Blue "Enable" link).C. The "Health Timeline" (Final Intelligence)While you started this in Phase 3, Phase 5 adds the Intelligence Layer.Filtering Sidebar: A clean list of checkboxes (Records, Appointments, Notes) in the left margin.Search Bar: A simple black-outlined input at the top of the timeline: "Search history..."Summary Tooltip: Hovering over a timeline node reveals a minimalist white pop-over with a brief summary of that event.3. Navigation & System HardeningAdmin Toggle: If the user is an Admin, a simple "Admin Mode" toggle appears in the sidebar.Rate Limiting UI: If a user is temporarily blocked due to too many failed logins, show a minimalist "Cooldown" screen: A large black clock icon and a message: "Too many attempts. Please wait 15 minutes."4.
