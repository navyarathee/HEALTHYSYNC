Phase 4 Design Doc: Collaboration & Virtual Care1. Visual Strategy: "Focus & Context"Background: Solid White (#FFFFFF).Typography: Slate Black for content; Navy Blue for metadata (e.g., "Dr. Aris posted...").Dividers: Minimalist 1px gray lines to separate distinct medical notes.Interactivity: Hover states use a very light blue tint to indicate "clickable" areas without adding permanent color.2. Core Modules & ComponentsA. Multi-Provider Case Notes (The "Sync" Feed)Instead of a "chat" (which looks messy), we use a Professional Note Feed.The Component: A vertical list of white cards with 1px gray borders.Header: Small Blue text for the Provider's Name and Specialty (e.g., Dr. Smith • Oncology).Body: Slate Black text for the clinical observation.Actions: Minimalist icons (Edit/Delete) that only appear on hover to keep the UI clean.B. Teleconsultation Interface (The "Virtual Room")Since this is a mock integration, the UI should feel "ready" for video.The Layout: A split-screen approach.Left (60%): A large Black placeholder for the Video Feed with a "Camera Off" icon.Right (40%): A White panel for "Quick Notes" so the provider can type while "talking."Control Bar: A floating bottom bar with three simple icons: Mute, End Call (Red/White), and Toggle Video.C. Collaborative Invitation ModalDesign: A minimalist white modal that appears when a doctor wants to "invite" a specialist to a case.Search: A simple blue-bordered input field to "Search by Provider Name or ID."Success State: A thin blue banner at the top: "Access granted to Dr. Miller."3. Navigation & Context"In-Case" Navigation: When a provider is viewing a patient, add a small tab-bar under the patient's name:Timeline | Medical Records | Collaboration (3) | AppointmentsBlue Indicators: Use a small blue dot (notification) next to the "Collaboration" tab if there are new unread notes from other doctors.4. Logic & Data FlowReal-time Simulation: Use a "Refresh" button or short-polling (fetching every 30 seconds) to simulate seeing other providers' notes.Context Linking: Ensure that when a note is written during a Teleconsult session, it is automatically tagged with a "Virtual Visit" badge in the main timeline.📊 Phase 4 Component InventoryComponentUI ElementsVisual PriorityCase Note CardWhite card, Blue Header, Black text.HighVideo PlaceholderSolid Black background, Center icon.HighInvitation ModalCenter-aligned, Blue primary button.MediumNew Note BadgeTiny Blue circle (no text).Low
